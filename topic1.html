Web Design in the Late 1990s

In 1999, web design was a struggle against limited bandwidth and primitive browsers. Developers lacked CSS for positioning, which led to the "Table Era." By using invisible HTML table tags, designers could force text and images into columns, creating a grid-like appearance that wouldn't break on different monitors.

As seen in the Amazon layout above, the design is incredibly text-dense. This was intentional; since high-quality images took minutes to load over dial-up, text was the primary way to engage users. Navigation was often a sidebar of plain blue links, and the concept of "User Experience" was rarely considered—the goal was simply to make the site functional and informative.
